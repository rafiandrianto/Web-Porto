My Journey

My fascination with tech started the moment I got my hands on my first personal computer. Over time, that curiosity naturally turned into a habit of diving deep into raw data, tinkering with patterns, and figuring out how things work under the hood.

That passion led me to pursue a degree in Information System Science and Engineering. The real turning point came during university when I built my first machine learning project. Seeing how algorithms transform messy inputs into accurate forecasts instantly hooked me on the power of predictive technology.

Since then, I’ve focused on the intersection of Data Science, Analytics, and Machine Learning. I love solving operational puzzles whether that’s predicting revenue risks, engineering clean software solutions, or building executive dashboards. Moving forward, I’m looking to bring these data-driven solutions to high-impact teams and real-world challenges.
